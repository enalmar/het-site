What happens inside HET.

It lists what it found, by name

Open What's included and every kind of data HET can read from your Health is there: heart rate, sleep, weight, blood pressure, steps and the rest. It covers all 206 kinds iOS 27 offers apps, except clinical health records.

It builds the zip on your phone

Workouts first, then every other kind of data, month by month. Nothing is uploaded anywhere while it runs. Keep HET open until it finishes: Health cannot be read while the phone is locked.

It tells you before the free part runs out

If an export reaches past the free 100 workouts and 100 days, HET says how much is past it before it starts. Export the free part, newest first, or unlock the rest. Never a surprise at the end.

Then it hands you the zip

Share it to Files, AirDrop or anywhere the share sheet reaches. If something could not be read, the finished screen lists it rather than leaving it out quietly.

Two reasons to take it all with you.

One export serves both. The workouts are ready to upload, and nothing about the rest has been thrown away.

What it will not do, and why.

Some are Apple's rules and some are choices made on purpose. Worth knowing before you start.

  • Write to Health

    HET only reads. It never asks to write, so it cannot change, add to or delete anything in your Health data.

  • Read clinical health records

    Records from hospitals and clinics are left alone. HET never asks for Health Records access.

  • Export history you did not share

    iOS asks how much history to share. Choose Past 30 Days and only 30 days can be exported. Choose All Recorded Data.

  • Read prescriptions without asking each time

    Apple shares vision prescriptions and medications one record at a time, through its own picker. HET reads only the ones you choose there.

Nothing leaves your iPhone until you share it.

There is no account to make and no server to send anything to. HET reads Health, writes the zip on your phone, and hands it to you. The only thing it ever asks the network is Apple's App Store, about the one-off unlock.

Before you start

Does HET change anything in Health?

No. It only reads. It never asks for permission to write, so nothing in Health can be changed, added or deleted by it.

Why does my export only go back 30 days?

When HET first asked to read Health, iOS offered Past 30 Days or All Recorded Data, and Past 30 Days was chosen. Change it in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › HET, then export again. The support page has the details.

What exactly is in the export?

Everything Apple Health lets an app read: all 206 kinds of data iOS 27 offers, except clinical health records. Workouts go in Workouts as FIT files, with routes and heart rate. Everything else goes in Health Data, one folder per kind, as CSV files with a readme explaining every column.

Where can I upload the workouts?

Anywhere that imports FIT files, which includes Garmin Connect, Strava, Intervals.icu and TrainingPeaks. Each has its own upload page; the support page says where to find them.

Is it a backup I could restore from?

It keeps everything needed to restore it. Every row carries Health's own record ID, the app and device that recorded it, and the rest of what Health stored with it, in plain files you can read without HET.

Where does the zip go?

Only where you send it. HET writes it into its own temporary storage on your phone, then hands it to the share sheet. HET deletes its copy the next time you export or open the app, and iOS may clear it sooner.

Can I open the health data without HET?

Yes. They are plain CSV files, which Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets all open. All times are in UTC, and each column header names its unit.

Can I get my money back?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Apple's process is at reportaproblem.apple.com. The free allowance exists so you can see the export of your own data first.
